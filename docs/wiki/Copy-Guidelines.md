# Copy Guidelines

Rules and decisions governing the site's written content.

## Geographic References

**Decision (2026-04-25):** All "Broward County" geographic references have been removed from the site.

**What was kept:**
- "Broward Partnership for the Homeless" — proper organization name (partner org)
- "Broward County, Florida" — legal jurisdiction clause in Terms & Conditions only

**Replacement patterns used:**
| Before                                    | After                              |
|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| "families in Broward County"              | "families in our community" / "families in need" |
| "children in Broward County"              | "children in need"                 |
| "Years Serving Broward County"            | "Years Serving Our Community"      |
| "Every dollar stays in Broward County"    | "Every dollar stays local"         |
| "Building Stronger Communities in Broward County" | "Building Stronger Communities" |
| "Broward County nonprofit" (SEO keyword)  | "South Florida nonprofit"          |

## Tax-Deductible Language

UNCS Cares Foundation is a 501(c)(3). Donations **are** tax-deductible — never say "may be."

## Tone

- Direct, warm, community-focused
- No emoji in graphics — emoji are acceptable as decorative icons in drive cards only
- CTO-level design sophistication — clean layouts, no clutter
- Humor goes in copy, not visuals

## Organization Name

Always "UNCS Cares Foundation" — never abbreviate to "UCF" or "UNCS Cares" in formal contexts.
