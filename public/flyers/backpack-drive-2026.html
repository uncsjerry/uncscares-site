Every Backpack Changes a School Year

Thousands of children in Broward County start the school year without basic supplies. For families experiencing homelessness or financial hardship, a backpack full of supplies isn't just helpful — it's transformative.

UNCS employees don't just write checks. We personally pack every backpack and deliver them to the children who need them most.
